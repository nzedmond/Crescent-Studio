# Crescent Studio brand language

Source: *Crescent Studio Description* (Nov 2026), elevator pitch, and lean canvas. Site copy lives in `src/data/content.js`; keep it consistent with this page.

## Positioning

> Crescent Studio is an external creative team for early-stage founders. We build your brand, produce your visual content, and tell your story, so you can stay focused on building the business.

**Tagline:** You focus on building the company. We build the story around it.

**Short form:** An external creative team for early-stage founders.

## Who we talk to

Early-stage founders who have a strong product or idea but no in-house creative capacity:

1. **Student founders**: building alongside classes, research, or a job; small team, tight budget.
2. **Time-constrained founders**: the company is running; creative work keeps losing to urgent priorities.
3. **Founders approaching a milestone**: launch, raise, new market; they need to be taken seriously.
4. **Founders who've tried other routes**: have used freelancers, agencies, or a hire, and know what frustrated them.

## The problem we name

Founders are busy with product, customers, fundraising, and operations, so the brand gets pushed aside. The company is often stronger than the way it looks and communicates. Hiring a full creative team is too expensive and too early.

## How we work (the four promises)

1. **We set the priorities.** Founders shouldn't have to work out which creative services they need. We identify the priorities and take responsibility for executing them.
2. **We know the founder's week.** Creative support without another complicated responsibility to manage.
3. **One creative direction.** No coordinating separate freelancers for branding, film, and photography.
4. **We grow with you.** From establishing an identity, to launching, building awareness, and growing market presence.

## Voice

- **Founder to founder.** Speak to "you" (the founder) as "we" (the team). Plain, direct, warm.
- **Relief, not hype.** The benefit is time and focus returned to the founder. Show how we remove work, not how impressive we are.
- **Human and documentary.** Real people, real moments, honest images. Avoid "stunning", "world-class", "cutting-edge".
- **Short sentences.** Lead with the outcome; one idea per sentence.

**Say:** external creative team, creative partner, one creative direction, your story, founders, early-stage, launch, raise.
**Avoid:** agency (about ourselves), content machine, synergy, leverage, solutions, "elevate your brand".
